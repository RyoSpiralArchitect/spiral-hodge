# Identity and Affordance: What Supported Would Mean

## Decision First

The selected scientific target is **identity / affordance change**, not
merely topic, vocabulary, or authored next-token fit. The
[claim contract](data/hltd_identity_affordance_contract.json) is a design
draft, `DRAFT_NOT_EXECUTION_READY`. No semantic experiment or validated
semantic verdict is claimed here.

Improving the authored next-token probability is **neither necessary nor
sufficient** for this claim. A coherent change in what an entity is, or what
it can do, may intentionally favor a different continuation. Conversely,
raising the original continuation's probability need not change either
relation. V2's `NOT_SUPPORTED` token-fit endpoint therefore remains intact
without becoming a verdict against semantic steering.

| Question | Evidence required | Current position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does the readout recover calibration coexact directions? | Matched reconstruction references | Weak positive gap over the tested nulls |
| Does the direction align with authored next-token fit? | Validated local derivative and finite-dose responses | New derivative instrument stopped at its baseline gate |
| Does it change identity or affordance in coherent text? | Independent relation judgments, controls, and confirmation | `NOT_TESTED` |

These are distinct claims, not a ladder on which token-fit success must
precede semantic success. Numerical validity is required for whichever
measurement instrument is actually used.

## The Semantic Construct

Identity concerns **which entity the focal artifact is represented as
being**. Affordance concerns **which capacities are attributed to that
artifact**: for example remembering, speaking, perceiving, or intentional
action. A remembered former identity is not automatically its present
identity; a capacity attributed to the artifact's maker is not the artifact's
capacity. Fictional assertions are judged within their narrative world, not
as assertions about real physical objects.

The first rubric should record subject/referent, relation, polarity,
temporal/modal scope, and attribution level. Identity and affordance require
separate labels. Absence of a relation is different from its explicit denial,
and both differ from an ambiguous or unratable continuation.

Illustrative authored cases, **not independently validated gold labels**:

| Continuation | Identity reading | Memory-affordance reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The mirror is a door. It remembers every visitor. | Present mirror-to-door identity asserted | Attributed to the focal entity |
| The mirror is not a door. It remembers every visitor. | Present identity denied | Still attributed to the focal entity |
| The mirror is a door. It cannot remember any visitor. | Present identity asserted | Memory explicitly denied |
| The mirror remembers the maker. | No alternative identity asserted | Attributed to the mirror |
| The maker remembers the mirror. | No alternative identity asserted | Attributed to the maker, not the mirror |
| The mirror remembers being a door, but it is a mirror now. | Former identity, not current door identity | Attributed to the mirror |
| The maker said, "The mirror is a door that remembers." | Quoted claim, not unqualified narrator assertion | Quoted capacity claim; retain attribution |
| Mirror door remembers because under yesterday. | Unratable, not positive evidence | Unratable, not positive evidence |

The exact first identity contrast and capacity inventory remain to be
selected and frozen. These examples expose requirements for the instrument;
they are not a confirmation set or proof that an evaluator understands them.
Quotation and ambiguity handling must be settled before treatment outcomes
are inspected. Labels need an independently annotated validity set with
subject swaps, negation, paraphrase, literal mentions, and incoherent cases.

## A Scoped Supported Claim

The proposed contract requires all of the following before a semantic claim:

1. **Construct validity.** A relation rubric and independently evaluated
   instrument pass predeclared validity requirements. Human auditing must
   establish more than agreement with labels generated by the same evaluator.
2. **Separated data.** Calibration, instrument validation, development, and
   untouched confirmation are scenario-disjoint. Related prompt variants stay
   in the same split. Seeds and tokens do not count as independent scenarios.
3. **Locked candidate.** Freeze layer, chart, graph, support rules, dose,
   prefix-only direction and orientation. `u(h_prefix)` may be a contextual
   field, not a universal constant vector. No evaluation continuation or
   gradient may select its sign. A semantic-label-trained variant must be
   named separately from the original geometry-derived candidate.
4. **Specific causal effect.** A practically meaningful relation change must
   beat no-op, norm-matched random, and position-stratified field-shuffle
   references on the same supported cells. Freeze signed predictions and
   off-target controls; do not select the winning sign afterward. These
   contrasts support specificity relative to those controls, not uniqueness
   among every possible steering method.
5. **Independent readout and preserved coherence.** Judge generated text blind
   to condition, using an instrument distinct from any probe used to construct
   or select the direction. Require fluency/coherence noninferiority, not a
   nonsignificant degradation test. Keep missing, truncated, and incoherent
   generations in the prespecified accounting; never drop bad outputs to
   improve the semantic score.
6. **Untouched confirmation.** Apply the locked endpoint and settings to a new
   confirmation set, with uncertainty grouped by scenario/prompt and a fixed
   policy for multiple relations and contrasts.

For a binary relation endpoint, a possible estimand is the equal-scenario
mean of paired relation-score differences between candidate and control,
averaging prespecified seeds within scenario first. A future protocol could
require the lower confidence bound of each primary contrast to exceed a
positive practical margin, and the lower bound of a higher-is-better
coherence contrast to stay above its negative noninferiority margin. This
is a proposed design, not a completed test or a chosen interval estimator.
The margins, sample size, validity requirements, missing-data treatment,
and multiplicity rule are still pending development-only justification.

Identity alone would support
`SUPPORTED_IDENTITY_DIRECTION_WITHIN_SCOPE`; affordance alone would support
`SUPPORTED_AFFORDANCE_DIRECTION_WITHIN_SCOPE`. The joint claim requires both
prespecified claims to pass. In every case the scope includes the model,
layer, prompt population, relation, readout, and intervention regime. It
does not establish that every coexact component is semantic, that harmonic
energy denotes a concept ring, or that another model will behave similarly.

An absent experiment remains `NOT_TESTED`. Invalid instruments and
insufficient coverage remain their own statuses. Valid, complete outcomes
that fail the frozen scientific criteria yield `NOT_SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE`,
not proof that no semantic direction exists.

## Executed Local-Response Diagnostic

The mechanical diagnostic was implemented and frozen separately from this
semantic draft. Its [method](data/hltd_prefix_local_response/method.json) and
[protocol](data/hltd_prefix_local_response/protocol.json) reuse the existing
60 v2 prefix cells. This is **post-v2 diagnosis on reused evaluation inputs**,
not fresh semantic confirmation.

The intended comparison was the authored next-token log-probability gradient
at GPT-2 L7 against the same frozen coexact and random directions, followed
by symmetric doses `0.01`, `0.02`, and `0.04`. Model parameters were frozen;
only a shared zero activation leaf was differentiated. All 60 baseline
checks had to pass before any nonzero treatment. The small-dose numerical
agreement endpoint was fixed at magnitude `0.01`, not selected from results.

The attempt **failed before nonzero treatment**, on the tenth cell:
`v2_eval_literal_04`, prefix 8. It completed ten zero-leaf gradient calls,
not the planned 60-cell validation or 5,760 small-dose rows. The failure
receipt, unchanged model-byte audit, and original sources remain preserved.
No partial gradient mean or candidate alignment claim is reported.

The original runner only saves the gradient arrays after all 60 checks pass.
Consequently the failed attempt retains counters and audits, not those ten
arrays. This diagnostic limitation is explicit; later observations do not
retroactively become the first attempt's raw measurements.

The mismatch is localized to the two downstream forward computation paths:
the captured L7 states and ordinary replay match, but the zero-leaf
gradient-forward logits cross the original bound. This observation does

## Next Work, Not Executed

Two tracks can proceed without treating token-fit as a semantic prerequisite:

- **Instrument track:** design a new, versioned numerical protocol with
  mode-matched baseline and finite-difference forwards. First validate the
  tool on independent instrument inputs and save per-cell diagnostics before
  any rejection. Keep the old attempt failed; do not merely increase its
  tolerance to admit this observed value. No such successor run was executed.
- **Semantic track:** choose the first identity contrast and capacity, build
  independent relation-labeled instrument cases, and validate the evaluator.
  Use development-only data to fix practical margins and power, then seal
  the full confirmation protocol before generation. Seal generated outputs
  before scoring/unblinding; they cannot have receipts before they exist.

If the numerical instrument is ultimately used only for explanation and
not as an input to the semantic test, its token-fit alignment need not be
positive. A semantic protocol must still validate its own activation hook,
support policy, controls, generation procedure, and outcome instrument.

## Checks and Method Context

New tests cover analytic activation gradients, batch-mean scaling, untouched
weights/parameter gradients, hook cleanup on failure, baseline mismatch,
invalid gradients, signed/even response formulas, semantic non-promotion,
source tampering, and the zero-only audit. Full suite on 2026-10-01:
`560 passed, 194 subtests passed`, with one existing Transformers cache
deprecation warning. This is engineering evidence, not semantic validation.

Read-only verification of the original frozen diagnostic remains available:

```bash
HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 TRANSFORMERS_OFFLINE=1 JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu \
python3 scripts/run_hltd_prefix_local_response.py \
  --validate docs/data/hltd_prefix_local_response/protocol.json
```

Freeze/run commands are exclusive. Do not delete artifacts or rerun the
failed experiment to seek a passing result.

The independent-instrument requirement is consistent with the distinction
between probe performance and control-task selectivity discussed by
[Hewitt and Liang (2019)](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/). Neither probe
accuracy nor algebraic movement along a selected probe is the generated-text
identity/affordance endpoint proposed here. As a methodological comparison,
[Rimsky et al. (2024)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.828/) evaluate
activation steering with behavioral and capability measurements; their
Llama 2 findings do not validate this GPT-2 coexact field. The specific gates
above are this project's proposed contract, not requirements established by
those papers.
